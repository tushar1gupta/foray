How we work with you.

Recruiters have earned their reputation. These are the things we do differently, and you can hold us to all three.

We name the company

No blind pitches about “a well-funded startup”. You get the company, the role and the team before you decide whether you are interested.

Comp up front

We tell you the range at the start, not after two interviews. If it is below what you told us you wanted, we will not waste your afternoon.

You never chase us

We chase the client for feedback and pass it on either way, including a no. Silence is the thing engineers hate most about recruiters and it is the easiest to fix.
