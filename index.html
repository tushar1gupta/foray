How It Works

        

          
Three Steps, Either Side Of The Table.

          

            
            
          

        

      


      

        

          

            
01 Introductory call

            
We learn the role from the engineer who will review the code

            
Sixty minutes on the stack, the seniority band, and what a strong hire looks like here.

          

          

            
02 Targeted outreach

            
We approach a named list, not a job board

            
People already doing this work at your stage, most of whom are not applying anywhere.

          

          

            
03 Introductions

            
Five engineers, ranked, with the code review attached

            
Salary, notice period, and work authorization on the first page.

          

        

      


      

        

          

            
01 Opportunity surfaced

            
We bring you the role, not a pipeline

            
One company at a time, with the stage, the team, and the comp band stated up front.

          

          

            
02 Fit assessed

            
A senior engineer reads your work

            
You get the written feedback either way, and we tell you honestly if it is not a match.

          

          

            
03 Introduction

            
You meet the founder or the hiring engineer

            
No screening rounds with people who cannot answer technical questions.

          

        

        
We ask about timeline, location and remote preference, visa status, and the
          comp band you would move for before anything goes to a company. Nothing is shared without
          your say.

What We Do

        

          
We Specialize In Early And Mid-Level Engineering Hires.

          
Five people who can do the work, not five who match a keyword.

        

      

      

        

          Early hires
          
Seed to Series A

          
Founding and first-team engineers who have shipped something end to end with nobody
            checking on them.

        

        

          Mid-level hires
          
Series B through growth

          
Engineers with three to eight years who have owned a system other teams depended on, and
            want more scope than their current title allows.
